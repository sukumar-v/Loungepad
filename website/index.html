Controllers

Any controller. Every button drawn as yours.

Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controllers all work, and so do a keyboard and mouse. The buttons on screen always match the controller in your hands — pick up a PlayStation controller and the green A becomes a cross.

The buttons, as they look on this controller
Plug in a controller and press any button: this page will show its buttons, and you can browse it from the couch.
Colour · Ember
Pick a colour for the app. Go on, try one here.
LS The left stick is the mouse.Slow and precise near the centre, fast when you push all the way. Set the speed to suit you.
AB Left and right click.Click anything in Windows. The right stick scrolls.
XBOX Xbox button: the Power Wheel.Tap it from anywhere, even mid-game, to switch windows, close what's stuck or go to sleep. Hold it to come back to Loungepad.
VIEW View: the on-screen keyboard.Type a search, a password or a message. Text boxes bring it up on their own.
The pointer gets out of the way.Use the D-pad and the pointer hides. Move the stick and it's back.
Y Any button, any shortcut.Give an app's shortcut a button of its own — Y for a new browser tab, say.
PAD The touchpad works like a laptop's.Tap to click, two fingers to scroll, pinch to zoom.
↑↓←→ Arrow keys move.A keyboard and mouse work too, and the mouse can click anything.
Enter Enter selects, Esc goes back.Right-click a game to open its menu.
XY X and Y are just X and Y.The buttons on screen turn into keys the moment you start typing.
/ Slash searches.Start typing a game's name and jump straight to it.
Tab Tab opens Settings.Everything can be changed from there.
Get started

On the couch in a minute.

There's nothing else to install. Download it, open it, and answer a few quick questions with your controller while it finds your games.

Free and open source Keeps itself up to date
01 Download LoungepadThe latest version, free. XDownload
02 Open LoungepadIt’s one file. Keep it somewhere of your own, like a folder in Documents, so it can keep itself up to date.
03 If Windows shows a warningWindows can be cautious about new apps the first time they open. More info → Run anyway
04 Set up from the couchPick your TV, your look and your game stores with the controller while it finds your games. Connecting your stores lets Loungepad show every game you own, not just the installed ones. Recommended
05 That's itLoungepad updates itself from then on. To remove it, just delete Loungepad.exe. Auto-update
